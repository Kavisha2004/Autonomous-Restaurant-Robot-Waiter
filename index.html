Automated Dining System Workflow

End-to-end automation from table-side QR ordering to autonomous robotic delivery.

1
📱

1. Table QR Order

Customer scans their table's unique QR link, selects menu items, inputs custom notes, and places their order.

2
👨‍🍳

2. Kitchen Preparation

Kitchen display automatically rings with a live audio chime. Staff prepare the food and mark the order as ready.

3
🤖

3. Robot Dispatch

Staff press Send Robot. The autonomous WaiterBot navigates along the floorplan to the exact target table.

4
✨

4. Delivery & Return

Customer unloads food and confirms receipt. WaiterBot autonomously navigates back to the home dock station.

5
⭐

5. Customer Feedback

Guests rate food quality and robot delivery speed at the end of their meal. Ratings stream directly to the kitchen.
